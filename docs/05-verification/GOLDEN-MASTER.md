# Source characterization and golden evidence

**Owner:** Collection of source evidence; comparison semantics belong to [COMPATIBILITY-SPEC](COMPATIBILITY-SPEC.md). At least three repetitions from verified independent reset profile produce variability candidates, not automatic semantic masks.

Pin sealed snapshot/profile, actor/fixture/clock and instrumentation identities. Record raw distinctions, qualified DB/session/cache/files/effects, causal envelope and declared completion boundary. Source itself can be inconsistent: conflicting repetitions remain uncertainty until a reviewed contract explains them. Generated IDs use relational mapping; CSRF/session tokens require lifecycle tests; business timestamps require boundary-clock scenarios. Random ordering/external variation must not become unordered/ignored merely because it varies.

Golden observations are finite samples with provenance, not universal truth. Corpus completeness is independently reviewed operational boundary evidence. Candidate normalization needs immutable decision refs, negative mutation tests and Gate 2 policy approval. Missing safe production-only behavior remains UNKNOWN/UNSUPPORTED and visibly limits scope.
