Zero setup means portable installation of specified interfaces only; legacy runtimes/images/fixtures/safe operational inputs still require setup. Offline replay requires previously acquired pinned bytes.

Assessment is the optional successful stopping checkpoint in this same M0–M6 workflow, not a separate engine or roadmap. [PROJECT-LIFECYCLE](../02-domain/PROJECT-LIFECYCLE.md) owns its independent completion/feasibility and selection semantics; TESTING X41–X55 gates the relevant milestone claims.
